When eyes first met

We met at a relative’s house,
where laughter spilled like sunlight.
You cherished my giggles,
and I fed you with my hands,
as if the world had paused
just for us,
our eyes meeting softly,
whispering that love had always known us.

Time seemed to bend around our smiles,
every word a secret,
every touch a quiet promise.
Even the air held its breath,
listening to the rhythm of our hearts,
as if the universe had paused
to witness our small, perfect moment.

Is this love?
Every time your eyes meet mine,
my world stops.
My heart flutters like wings in the wind.
Are you the one?
My soul delights in you,
and remembers the sunlight,
the warmth of your gaze,
the memory of laughter
that lingers like a gentle song.
